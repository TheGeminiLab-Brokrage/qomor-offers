Unit pin toolplace one dot per unit

Click the middle of each unit on the drawing. The highlighted code in the list is the one being placed; it advances automatically. Work is saved in this browser as you go. Paste the output into PLANS[floor].pins in js/plan.js.

Tracing the shape
Drag a corner to move it — this is the fast way to adjust a seeded outline.
Click adds a corner at the end · Shift-click inserts one into the nearest edge · Alt-click deletes the corner under the pointer · Backspace takes back the last · Enter finishes.
The shape belongs to the selected building and floor. Paste the output into FOCUS_SHAPES in js/plan.js.
